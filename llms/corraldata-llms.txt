# CorralData

> CorralData is a HIPAA-compliant AI analytics platform that connects 600+ data sources — EMRs, CRMs, ad platforms, POS, and billing systems — into a single, governed source of truth. Its AI agent, AskCorral, answers any business question and can act on the data, backed by white-glove onboarding with a dedicated Technical Account Manager instead of a DIY setup. Rated 4.9 on G2. CorralData serves data-driven teams in MedSpa/aesthetics, dental, behavioral health, plastic surgery, veterinary, private equity, retail, and ecommerce.

## Platform

- [The Whole Stack](https://corraldata.com/platform/): How CorralData works end to end — data unified, modeled, and turned into action.
- [AskCorral Agent](https://corraldata.com/askcorral-agent/): AI reporting and AI agents that answer any business question and act on your data, not just analyze it.
- [Instant Insights](https://corraldata.com/instant-insights/): Automated, plain-language answers surfaced from your connected data without being asked.
- [Data Apps](https://corraldata.com/data-apps/): Governed, live tools built on your data.
- [Integrations](https://corraldata.com/integrations/): 600+ prebuilt connectors for EMRs, CRMs, ad platforms, POS, and billing systems — no engineering required.
- [Reverse ETL](https://corraldata.com/reverse-etl/): AI agents detect opportunities in your data and sync them back across your platforms.
- [Roll-up Reporting](https://corraldata.com/roll-up-reporting/): Sync data from 600+ sources, align KPIs, and see portfolio-wide results across every location.
- [CorralData MCP](https://corraldata.com/corraldata-mcp/): Brings your business data and intelligence directly into Claude and other AI assistants.
- [Data Security](https://corraldata.com/data-security/): HIPAA-compliant security, including BAAs, so you can use AI without compromising compliance.
- [Guided Onboarding](https://corraldata.com/guided-onboarding/): A dedicated Technical Account Manager guides your team through white-glove, no-code setup.
